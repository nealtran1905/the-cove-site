How do I move my cove to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Settings from the gear and tap "Copy code" under Backup. Keep the code somewhere safe, such as a note or an email to yourself. On the new phone, open Settings, paste the code into the "Paste a backup code" box and tap "Restore".

If you bought the full cove, tap "Restore Purchases" in Settings on the new phone, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account. You won't be charged again.

What does unlocking the full cove include?

The game is free up to the cove's Recovering stage. The one $3.99 purchase opens the rest of the home cove: every find and cove stage after Recovering, up to Wild and the legend. You buy it once and keep it. Everything you found before buying stays yours either way.

The fogged parts of the coast map are not part of it. They come in later updates.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple or Google, so refunds do too, and I can't issue them myself.

  • iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Android: Google Play's refund page

Are the tides in the game real?

No. The game's tides follow your phone's clock, rising and falling about every 12 hours 25 minutes like a real tide, but they don't match any real shore. Before you go rock pooling, always check a real tide table and keep an eye on the sea.

Does the game need the internet?

No. It plays fully offline. Only buying or restoring the unlock talks to Apple or Google.
