The Cove Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

The Cove collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the game sends nothing to me or anyone else.

Who makes the game

The Cove is made by Hoang Tran, an independent developer. If you have a question about this policy, email nealtranuk@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress and settings are saved on your phone only. I cannot see them. If you delete the game, they are deleted too.

The backup code in Settings is your progress written as text. It is copied only when you tap "Copy code". Where you paste it is up to you.

Purchases

Unlocking the full cove, or a later area, is bought through the App Store or Google Play, and their own privacy policies apply. I never see your name, Apple ID, Google account or payment details. The game only remembers on your phone that the unlock was bought, and "Restore Purchases" asks Apple or Google to confirm it.

Adding a low tide to your calendar

When you tap "Add to calendar", the game makes a calendar event on your phone and opens the phone's own screen for adding it (on older iPhones, the share sheet). It does not read your calendar or ask for access to it.

Low tide reminders

In the iPhone app you can turn on a low tide reminder in Settings. It is off until you do, and the phone asks your permission then. The reminders are made on your phone and come at most once a day. Nothing is sent to me or anyone else, and you can turn them off in the game or in your phone's Settings.

Saving a page as a picture

When you tap "Save as a picture" on a page of the book, the game draws that page as a picture and opens your phone's share sheet. If you choose to save it to Photos, the phone asks your permission to add it. The game only adds the picture you chose. It never reads your photos.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

This website

This page has no cookies or analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to show you, correct or delete. Everything the game keeps is on your phone, and you can delete it by deleting the game.

Changes

If the game ever starts handling data differently, I will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
